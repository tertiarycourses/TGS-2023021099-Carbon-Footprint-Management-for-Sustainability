# Scenario — Activity 6: Prioritise a Reduction Portfolio

## Case context

A Singapore automotive-components supplier has six shortlisted initiatives and S$700,000 available in Wave 1. The case is inspired by public examples of enterprise footprint management; all decision data are illustrative.

## Learning purpose

Combine MACC, weighted criteria, dependencies and budget constraints into a transparent portfolio.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **SAP customer spotlight, CarbonChain and IMD** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

MACC, MCDA matrix, funded roadmap and decision record
