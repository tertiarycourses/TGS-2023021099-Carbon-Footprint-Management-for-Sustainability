# Activity 4: Model a Singapore Fleet Transition

**Real-use-case lens:** LionCity Logistics
**Source lens:** EMA grid statistics and GHG Protocol
**Objective:** Build business-as-usual and reduction scenarios for diesel, route optimisation and fleet electrification.
**Deliverable:** 2030 scenario model, assumptions register and recommendation

## Scenario

LionCity Logistics runs 40 diesel vans in Singapore. Customer demand is expected to grow 5% per year. Management is considering route optimisation, driver coaching and replacement of 20 vans with EVs by 2028.

## Detailed procedure

1. Review the fleet, distance, fuel and growth assumptions.
2. Calculate the 2025 diesel baseline in tCO₂e.
3. Project annual vehicle-kilometres to 2030 under business-as-usual.
4. Model route optimisation as an activity reduction.
5. Model driver coaching as an efficiency improvement.
6. Model EV electricity using 0.20 kWh/km and the stated grid-factor scenarios.
7. Create low, expected and high cases for adoption and performance.
8. Separate activity, efficiency, factor and structural effects.
9. Run sensitivity on annual distance and grid factor.
10. Recommend a phased roadmap with one decision trigger per phase.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
