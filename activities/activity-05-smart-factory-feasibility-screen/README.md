# Activity 5: Screen a Smart-Factory Carbon Initiative

**Real-use-case lens:** Matsumoto Precision-inspired teaching case
**Source lens:** SAP customer spotlight and Yurtay (2025)
**Objective:** Assess the technical, operational, data and financial feasibility of IoT/ERP-enabled energy optimisation.
**Deliverable:** Feasibility scorecard and pilot charter

## Scenario

Sakura Precision SG operates CNC machining, compressed air and heat-treatment equipment. Inspired by Matsumoto Precision’s public sustainability-footprint work, it is considering sub-metering, IoT sensors and ERP integration. All figures are illustrative.

## Detailed procedure

1. Map the energy and material flows for the selected production cell.
2. Identify decisions currently blocked by missing or delayed data.
3. Define the minimum sensors, tags and ERP master data needed.
4. Assess interoperability, cybersecurity, ownership and maintenance risks.
5. Estimate baseline energy, idle load and scrap-related emissions.
6. Define the intervention and control group or comparison period.
7. Calculate screened savings, cost and payback range.
8. Set quality, uptime and production guardrails.
9. Score technical, operational, financial and evidence feasibility.
10. Write a 12-week pilot charter with scale, modify and stop rules.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
