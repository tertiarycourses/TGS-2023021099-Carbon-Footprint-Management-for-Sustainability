# Scenario — Activity 1: Map a Multi-Site Corporate Inventory

## Case context

Merlion Retail Group operates 12 stores, one warehouse, a leased head office and a contracted delivery fleet. It owns 80% of a Malaysian sourcing subsidiary. Management wants a 2025 baseline before setting a target.

## Learning purpose

Define organisational and operational boundaries, classify sources and identify data gaps.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **GHG Protocol Corporate Standard** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Boundary memo, scope map and data-gap register
