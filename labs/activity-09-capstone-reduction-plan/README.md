# Activity 9: Build the Board-Ready Carbon Reduction Plan

**Real-use-case lens:** Lion City Precision Pte Ltd  
**Source lens:** Integrated course case using GHG Protocol, ISO/EMS and Singapore context  
**Objective:** Integrate hotspot analysis, prioritisation, governance and action planning into a defensible 2030 plan.  
**Deliverable:** Ten-slide board pack plus action register and management-review dashboard

## Scenario

Lion City Precision manufactures components in Singapore. Its 2025 screening footprint is 10,000 tCO₂e: purchased aluminium 42%, electricity 28%, natural gas 12%, freight 9%, waste 5% and other 4%. The board is considering a 35% absolute reduction by 2030 while production grows 4% annually.

## Detailed procedure

1. State the organisational and operational boundary and the 2025 baseline limitations.
2. Calculate source contributions and identify the top hotspots.
3. Build a 2030 business-as-usual scenario with 4% annual production growth.
4. Define a precise 2030 absolute target and interim milestones.
5. Evaluate the initiative long list using carbon, cash, feasibility, strategy and confidence.
6. Select and sequence a portfolio, checking dependencies and double counting.
7. Create an initiative register with owner, budget, milestone, KPI and M&V method.
8. Design governance using executive accountability, process owners and a carbon manager.
9. Create quarterly outcome, driver, delivery and data-quality KPIs.
10. Present the plan, defend its assumptions and record peer-review changes.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
