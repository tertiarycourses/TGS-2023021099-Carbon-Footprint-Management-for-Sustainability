# Scenario — Activity 9: Build the Board-Ready Carbon Reduction Plan

## Case context

Lion City Precision manufactures components in Singapore. Its 2025 screening footprint is 10,000 tCO₂e: purchased aluminium 42%, electricity 28%, natural gas 12%, freight 9%, waste 5% and other 4%. The board is considering a 35% absolute reduction by 2030 while production grows 4% annually.

## Learning purpose

Integrate hotspot analysis, prioritisation, governance and action planning into a defensible 2030 plan.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **Integrated course case using GHG Protocol, ISO/EMS and Singapore context** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Ten-slide board pack plus action register and management-review dashboard
