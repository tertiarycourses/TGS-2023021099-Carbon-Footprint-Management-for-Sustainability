# Scenario — Activity 4: Model a Singapore Fleet Transition

## Case context

LionCity Logistics runs 40 diesel vans in Singapore. Customer demand is expected to grow 5% per year. Management is considering route optimisation, driver coaching and replacement of 20 vans with EVs by 2028.

## Learning purpose

Build business-as-usual and reduction scenarios for diesel, route optimisation and fleet electrification.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **EMA grid statistics and GHG Protocol** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

2030 scenario model, assumptions register and recommendation
