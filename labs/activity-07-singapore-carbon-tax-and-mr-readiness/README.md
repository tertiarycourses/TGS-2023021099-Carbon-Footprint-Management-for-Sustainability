# Activity 7: Prepare a Singapore MR and Carbon-Tax Readiness Plan

**Real-use-case lens:** Jurong specialty-chemicals facility  
**Source lens:** NEA Carbon Tax and MR requirements  
**Objective:** Screen regulatory status, data controls, reporting obligations and carbon-price exposure.  
**Deliverable:** Obligations matrix, control plan and screened tax exposure

## Scenario

HarbourChem’s Jurong facility estimates 30,000 tCO₂e of annual direct emissions from natural gas, process emissions and refrigerants. Management needs a readiness review for the 2026 reporting year. This is an illustrative learning case, not legal advice.

## Detailed procedure

1. Read the current NEA carbon-tax and MR pages supplied in the Sources file.
2. Confirm the reporting year and the current threshold and rate used in the case.
3. Create a complete direct-emission source register.
4. Map each source to activity data, factor, method and evidence owner.
5. Screen annual tCO₂e and compare it with 2,000 and 25,000 tCO₂e thresholds.
6. Calculate gross carbon-tax exposure at the stated rate.
7. Draft a RACI for Designated Representative, GHG Manager, operations, finance and verifier.
8. Design capture, calculation, review, change-control and retention controls.
9. Build a calendar for monitoring-plan, verification and annual-report readiness.
10. Record legal and technical questions requiring current professional confirmation.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
