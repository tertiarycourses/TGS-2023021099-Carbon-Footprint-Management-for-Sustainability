# Scenario — Activity 7: Prepare a Singapore MR and Carbon-Tax Readiness Plan

## Case context

HarbourChem’s Jurong facility estimates 30,000 tCO₂e of annual direct emissions from natural gas, process emissions and refrigerants. Management needs a readiness review for the 2026 reporting year. This is an illustrative learning case, not legal advice.

## Learning purpose

Screen regulatory status, data controls, reporting obligations and carbon-price exposure.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **NEA Carbon Tax and MR requirements** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Obligations matrix, control plan and screened tax exposure
