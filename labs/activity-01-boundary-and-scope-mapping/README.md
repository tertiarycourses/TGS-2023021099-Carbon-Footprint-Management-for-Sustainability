# Activity 1: Map a Multi-Site Corporate Inventory

**Real-use-case lens:** Singapore retail and distribution group  
**Source lens:** GHG Protocol Corporate Standard  
**Objective:** Define organisational and operational boundaries, classify sources and identify data gaps.  
**Deliverable:** Boundary memo, scope map and data-gap register

## Scenario

Merlion Retail Group operates 12 stores, one warehouse, a leased head office and a contracted delivery fleet. It owns 80% of a Malaysian sourcing subsidiary. Management wants a 2025 baseline before setting a target.

## Detailed procedure

1. Read the scenario and identify the intended users of the inventory.
2. Draw the legal and operating structure, including leased and contracted activities.
3. Choose equity share, financial control or operational control; state the rationale.
4. Set the reporting period, gases and materiality rule.
5. Create a source register for stores, warehouse, office, subsidiary and logistics.
6. Classify sources by scope and relevant Scope 3 category.
7. Rate each source on magnitude, evidence quality and controllability.
8. Record exclusions, assumptions and the conditions that would trigger recalculation.
9. Peer-review the boundary memo against the five GHG Protocol principles.
10. Present the two highest-risk data gaps and an improvement action for each.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
