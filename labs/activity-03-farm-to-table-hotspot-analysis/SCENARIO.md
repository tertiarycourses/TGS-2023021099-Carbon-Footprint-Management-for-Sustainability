# Scenario — Activity 3: Find Hotspots in a Farm-to-Table Value Chain

## Case context

Orchid Foods produces chilled ready meals for Singapore retailers. Inspired by CP Foods’ farm-to-table footprint work, it wants a first screening of ingredients, factory energy, cold-chain logistics, packaging and waste. All figures are illustrative teaching data, not reported CP Foods results.

## Learning purpose

Calculate a screening footprint, rank hotspots and propose a data-improvement plan.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **SAP customer spotlight and GHG Protocol** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Calculated footprint, Pareto chart and hotspot recommendation
