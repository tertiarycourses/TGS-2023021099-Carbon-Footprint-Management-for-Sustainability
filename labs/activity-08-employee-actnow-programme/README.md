# Activity 8: Design an Employee ActNow Programme

**Real-use-case lens:** Singapore professional-services firm  
**Source lens:** United Nations ActNow  
**Objective:** Convert selected personal actions into a privacy-respecting corporate engagement programme with measurable outcomes.  
**Deliverable:** Programme canvas, measurement plan and communication copy

## Scenario

Marina Advisory has 450 employees and material Scope 3 emissions from commuting, business travel, purchased goods and food at events. Leaders want an eight-week ActNow campaign without overstating carbon savings.

## Detailed procedure

1. Review the firm’s screening footprint and identify controllable behaviour-linked sources.
2. Select three UN ActNow actions that connect to those sources.
3. Define one behaviour, one enabling policy and one barrier for each action.
4. Use the SG Carbon Calculator only for optional learning and directional reflection.
5. Define aggregate participation and operational outcome metrics.
6. Set a baseline using booking, travel, catering or waste data—not personal claims alone.
7. Design an eight-week cadence of prompt, enablement, feedback and recognition.
8. Write a privacy notice and a no-shaming participation principle.
9. Create a method to avoid double counting with formal reduction projects.
10. Draft a balanced results message showing actions, evidence and limitations.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
