# Scenario — Activity 8: Design an Employee ActNow Programme

## Case context

Marina Advisory has 450 employees and material Scope 3 emissions from commuting, business travel, purchased goods and food at events. Leaders want an eight-week ActNow campaign without overstating carbon savings.

## Learning purpose

Convert selected personal actions into a privacy-respecting corporate engagement programme with measurable outcomes.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **United Nations ActNow** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Programme canvas, measurement plan and communication copy
