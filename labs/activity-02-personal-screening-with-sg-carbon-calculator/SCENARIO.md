# Scenario — Activity 2: Use the SG Carbon Calculator as an Engagement Tool

## Case context

A 450-person professional-services firm wants an employee campaign covering commuting, home energy, food and waste. The sustainability lead proposes using the SG Carbon Calculator and adding all results to the corporate inventory.

## Learning purpose

Use personal-footprint screening responsibly, then translate behavioural insights into corporate data needs.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **SG Carbon Calculator and UN ActNow** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Calculator evidence, limitations note and engagement experiment
