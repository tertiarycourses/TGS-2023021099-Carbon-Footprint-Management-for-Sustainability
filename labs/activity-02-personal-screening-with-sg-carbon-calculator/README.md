# Activity 2: Use the SG Carbon Calculator as an Engagement Tool

**Real-use-case lens:** Employee carbon-literacy campaign  
**Source lens:** SG Carbon Calculator and UN ActNow  
**Objective:** Use personal-footprint screening responsibly, then translate behavioural insights into corporate data needs.  
**Deliverable:** Calculator evidence, limitations note and engagement experiment

## Scenario

A 450-person professional-services firm wants an employee campaign covering commuting, home energy, food and waste. The sustainability lead proposes using the SG Carbon Calculator and adding all results to the corporate inventory.

## Detailed procedure

1. Open https://alfredang.github.io/sgcarboncalculator/ in a modern browser.
2. Record a fictional persona; do not enter identifiable learner data.
3. Complete the energy, transport, food and waste inputs.
4. Capture the total and category breakdown in the provided worksheet.
5. Change one material input and observe the direction and scale of change.
6. Select two relevant UN ActNow actions and explain the mechanism of impact.
7. Map each action to corporate Scope 1, 2 or 3 influence.
8. List the calculator’s boundary, factor and verification limitations.
9. Design a four-week opt-in engagement experiment with one aggregate outcome metric.
10. Write a clear disclaimer separating personal screening from corporate accounting.

## Evidence checklist

- State the boundary, period, unit and source for every material figure.
- Separate calculated result, assumption and management judgement.
- Show formulas and retain intermediate values.
- Label company-linked figures as reported or illustrative.
- Record uncertainty, limitation and data-improvement actions.
- Ensure the final recommendation answers every scenario question.
