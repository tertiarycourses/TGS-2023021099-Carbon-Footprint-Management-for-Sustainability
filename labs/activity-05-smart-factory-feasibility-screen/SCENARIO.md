# Scenario — Activity 5: Screen a Smart-Factory Carbon Initiative

## Case context

Sakura Precision SG operates CNC machining, compressed air and heat-treatment equipment. Inspired by Matsumoto Precision’s public sustainability-footprint work, it is considering sub-metering, IoT sensors and ERP integration. All figures are illustrative.

## Learning purpose

Assess the technical, operational, data and financial feasibility of IoT/ERP-enabled energy optimisation.

## Source and evidence note

This activity uses **SAP customer spotlight and Yurtay (2025)** as its professional or real-use-case lens. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, all quantities, costs, emission factors and company circumstances in this activity are **illustrative teaching data**. They must not be presented as reported company performance.

## Required output

Feasibility scorecard and pilot charter
