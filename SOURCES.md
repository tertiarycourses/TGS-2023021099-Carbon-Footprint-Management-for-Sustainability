## Primary standards and Singapore requirements

- [GHG Protocol Corporate Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard) — corporate inventory principles, boundaries, scopes, calculation and reporting.
- [National Environment Agency — Carbon Tax](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/climate-change-energy-efficiency/climate-change/carbon-tax) — Singapore coverage, thresholds and current carbon-tax rates.
- [National Environment Agency — Measurement and Reporting Requirements](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/climate-change-energy-efficiency/climate-change/carbon-tax/measurement-and-reporting-requirements-for-greenhouse-gas-emissions) — reportable/taxable facility duties, monitoring, reporting and verification.
- [Energy Market Authority — Singapore Energy Statistics, Chapter 2](https://www.ema.gov.sg/resources/singapore-energy-statistics/chapter2) — electricity generation and published grid-emission factors.
- ISO 14001 and ISO 14064 concepts are taught at overview level; use licensed current standards for formal implementation or certification work.

## Source-use rules applied in this courseware

- Regulatory rates, thresholds and emission factors are dated and must be verified before external reporting.
- Public company examples provide a real-use-case lens; teaching datasets are explicitly labelled illustrative.
- The SG Carbon Calculator is used for personal screening and engagement, not as a substitute for a controlled corporate inventory.
- Reported findings from reviews and vendor pages are presented as examples, not independent proof of company-wide outcomes.
