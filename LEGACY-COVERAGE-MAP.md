# Legacy v6 Coverage and v7 Expansion Map

The 221-slide legacy deck was reviewed before the v7 rebuild. Its essential competency content has been retained and reorganised into a 133-slide, concept-led visual narrative.

| Legacy content area | v7 treatment |
|---|---|
| Sustainability, climate change, SDGs, ESG and Singapore context | Condensed into decision context, standards, Singapore carbon tax and action planning |
| ISO 14001, ISO 14064 and environmental management systems | Expanded across K1 and K2 with a clear accounting-standard versus management-system distinction |
| GHG Protocol, scopes and carbon calculations | Expanded with five principles, organisational/operational boundaries, seven gases, data quality and calculation controls |
| Hotspots and carbon calculators | Expanded into a hotspot-analysis funnel, Pareto/materiality logic and the authentic SG Carbon Calculator activity |
| Baselines, projections, scenarios and MACC | Expanded with BAU, decomposition, uncertainty, rebound, NPV, marginal abatement cost and sensitivity |
| Technology, circularity, logistics, power and waste | Reorganised through the reduction hierarchy, operational levers, Industry 4.0/ERP, supplier engagement and pilot gates |
| Prioritisation tools | Expanded through MCDA, scoring anchors, dependencies, portfolio waves and bias checks |
| Carbon tax and Singapore reporting | Updated from current NEA sources with explicit reportable/taxable thresholds and 2026–2027 rate |
| Carbon reduction plan | Expanded with target design, KPI tree, governance, RACI, initiative register, stage gates, M&V and management review |
| Legacy activities | Replaced by nine connected real-use-case activities, each with its own folder, scenario, questions, data and detailed procedure |

The v7 slide deck deliberately excludes step-by-step activity procedures. Those procedures are maintained in each `labs/activity-*` folder and in the Learner Guide.
